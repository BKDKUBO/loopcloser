# LoopCloser: 5 questions for a clinician (one page)

LoopCloser helps a rural health unit follow up abnormal screening results: each result gets an owner, a next
action and a deadline, and it escalates when nobody acts. **Rules decide; the software does not diagnose.**
Everything you see is fictional demo data. Your answers are recorded in `docs/clinical-review.md`.
